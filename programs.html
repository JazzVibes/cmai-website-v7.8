Respect first

Bow on and off the mat, thank your partner, and use controlled contact unless instructed otherwise.

Safety culture

Tap early, listen closely, and expect coaches to pause drills when technique or pace slips.

Parent friendly

Parents can watch class and get one or two focus items for practice at home.
